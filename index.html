How to Play 🎲

1
Take turns

Pass the device around. A big banner shows whose turn it is, in their color.

2
Spell It!

Look at the picture and tap 🔊 to hear the word. Tap the letter tiles to fill the boxes and spell it.

3
Change-a-Letter

Change just one letter to make a brand-new word — CAT → COT → COG. Stack up a long word ladder!

4
Win stars ⭐

Spell it right to earn points and stars. Stuck? Tap 💡 Hint. The winner has the most points!

💛
Everybody wins

Wrong tries are okay! You just get a gentle "try again" — never a buzzer. Learning is fun here.

Settings ⚙️

This game keeps no data about your family and never goes online. Everything stays private on this device. 💛
